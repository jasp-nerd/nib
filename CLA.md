## Why this exists

Nib is AGPL-3.0 and the intent is that it stays free software. The point of a CLA here is narrow:
without one, relicensing later needs the agreement of every contributor who ever landed a patch,
which in practice means it can never happen at all — not even to move to a *more* permissive
licence, or to a successor of the AGPL.

It does not let anyone take your contribution private and stop distributing the source. The
